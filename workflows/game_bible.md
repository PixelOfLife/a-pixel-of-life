# Game Bible: "A Pixel of Life"

## Premise
A life simulation RPG centered on emotional development and music. The player embodies a soul from conception, where early choices in the womb shape their core personality, but they retain the power to change throughout their life journey.

## Core Concepts

### 1. The Beginning (Womb Stage)
- **First Frame:** Pure sound - the heartbeat (the "Beat").
- **Atmosphere:** Abstract, floating, dreamlike. No gore, just abstract shapes, particles, and light.
- **Interaction:** The player interacts with "Vibrations" (muffled sounds from the outside world).
- **Choice System:**
  - Positive Vibrations (Mother's joy, music) -> "White Light" effects -> Develops Kindness/Empathy base stats.
  - Negative Vibrations (Stress, loud noises) -> "Dark Pulse" effects -> Develops Resilience/Defense base stats.
- **Result:** The "Core Personality" is formed. This doesn't lock you out of anything, but makes certain paths easier or harder (e.g., being "Kind" makes "Selfish" choices harder to execute later).

### 2. Emotional Development (The Progression)
- **Temperament:** Shaped by the Womb Stage choices. Acts as a passive modifier.
- **Emotional Intelligence (EQ):** The player's "XP" and "Skill Tree".
- **Superpowers:** EQ skills manifest as literal powers (e.g., "Calm" = Shield, "Empathy" = Healing, "Courage" = Strength).

### 3. The Antagonists (The Challenges)
- **Manifestations:** Abstract bosses representing negative emotions (The Shadow of Doubt, The Beast of Rage).
- **Combat Style:** Rhythm-based or Musical. You fight "Noise" with "Harmony".

### 4. Target Audience
- **Age:** 6+
- **Tone:** Clear, accessible, supportive, educational but fun.
- **Goal:** Help players recognize and manage their emotions.

## Music & Communication System

### The Heartbeat (El Latido)
The primary communication channel. The player's emotional state is reflected in the BPM:
- **Calm (80 BPM):** Tranquility, sleep, recovery.
- **Normal (120 BPM):** Neutral state, resting.
- **Happy (140 BPM):** Joy, excitement, positive stimulation.
- **Distressed (160 BPM):** Stress, pain, negative stimulation.

### Vibrations (Vibraciones)
The "language" of the womb. The mother/player sends messages that arrive as vibrations:
- **Light Vibrations (White):** Positive words (love, joy, beauty).
- **Dark Vibrations (Purple):** Negative words (fear, anger, sadness).
- **Soft Vibrations (Blue):** Calm words (sleep, peace, gentle).

### EQ Superpowers (Poderes de Inteligencia Emocional)
Emotional Intelligence skills act as the player's abilities in combat:
- **Calm:** Shield/Defense against Anxiety.
- **Empathy:** Healing/Connection with others.
- **Courage:** Strength/Attack power.
- **Joy:** Energy/Speed boost.
- **Patience:** Perception/Strategy buff.

## Visual Style
- **Womb:** Abstract, fluid shapes, particles, bioluminescence (Pixel Art style).
- **Life:** Varied, symbolic environments representing emotional states.
